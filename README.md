# CareBridge

## Overview

CareBridge is a cross-platform healthcare and community support application designed to improve the safety, independence, and quality of life of elderly people and individuals with disabilities. The platform connects elderly users, family members, and volunteers through a secure and accessible mobile application.

The project focuses on emergency response, health monitoring, assistance requests, medication reminders, and communication between users and caregivers.

---

## Features

### Elderly Users
- Secure account registration and login
- Emergency SOS button
- Voice emergency commands
- Medication and appointment reminders
- Assistance request management
- One-tap family calling
- Daily news with audio support
- Personal profile and health information

### Family Members
- Monitor user health status
- Live location tracking
- Safe zone monitoring
- Emergency notifications
- Reminder management
- Weekly health reports
- Medication tracking

### Volunteers
- Accept or reject assistance requests
- Task management
- User safety monitoring
- Reward and achievement system
- Ratings and feedback
- Availability management
